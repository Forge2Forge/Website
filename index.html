About the Show

From One Forge to the Next

Forge2Forge is where we sit down with men and talk about the forges of life God has taken them through: the losses, the failures, the battles and the hard seasons that shaped who they are today.

Iron doesn't get shaped in one shot. It goes into the fire, takes the heat and the hammer, and then goes back into the next forge. Every man has his own story of the fire. We're a couple of messed-up guys who don't have it all figured out either. We're here to hear those stories, tell them straight, and point it all back to God.

"Iron sharpeneth iron; so a man sharpeneth the countenance of his friend."Proverbs 27:17
